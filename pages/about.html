About This Report

Context and methodology behind the mission.

Mission Brief

This report summarises a three-day fact-finding mission to London Tech Week 2026 (June 8–10). The objective was to assess the current state of key technology sectors, identify emerging trends, and evaluate potential opportunities for strategic investment and partnership.

The mission covered AI (AI Arena, Applied AI, Intelligence 2.0), quantum computing (Quantum Showcase), climate tech (Planet & Life Sciences), fintech (Finance track), and deep science (Deep Tech Stage, SpaceTech & Robotics) — with a focus on technologies approaching commercial maturity.

Methodology

How the information was gathered and validated.

  • Session Attendance: 40+ talks, panels, and keynotes attended across the AI Arena, Core Stage, Founders Stage, Deep Tech Stage, and Ignition Stage
  • Startup Meetings: A random selection of early growth-stage startups across the Startup Village and expo floor
  • Masterclasses: Sessions on agentic AI, quantum computing, and enterprise resilience
  • Networking: London Tech Week Party and evening receptions at London's iconic venues
  • Quantum Showcase: Full afternoon dedicated to the UK's quantum strategy with IonQ, NVIDIA, BT, and UK government
  • Desk Research: Pre and post event analysis of the official 304-session agenda, company materials, and market reports
