Mission Brief

This report summarises a five-day fact-finding mission to London Tech Week 2026 (June 8–12). The objective was to assess the current state of key technology sectors, identify emerging trends, and evaluate potential opportunities for strategic investment and partnership.

The mission covered AI, climate tech, fintech, and deep science — with a focus on technologies that are approaching commercial maturity and have direct relevance to our strategic roadmap.

Methodology

How the information was gathered and validated.

  • Session Attendance: 40+ talks, panels, and keynotes attended across five concurrent tracks
  • Startup Meetings: 25 structured 30-minute meetings with early- to growth-stage startups
  • Workshops: 8 hands-on workshops on topics from AI safety to carbon accounting
  • Networking: 4 evening receptions and 2 dedicated networking sessions
  • Lab Tours: Site visits to the Francis Crick Institute and Oxford Ionics quantum lab
  • Desk Research: Pre- and post-event analysis of company materials, market reports, and regulatory filings
