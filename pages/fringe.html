The side events, workshops, receptions, and informal gatherings that complemented the main programme.

The AI Summit London

A headline event in its own right, co-located with LTW

Now in its 10th edition, The AI Summit London ran alongside the main festival on 10–11 June at Tobacco Dock in East London, with combo passes available for attendees wanting both events. It's a substantial event on its own terms — eight conference stages, an expected 4,500 attendees, and new tracks for Sustainable Innovation and Data Excellence added for 2026.

Beyond the stage content, the Summit ran an interactive "AI Trail" of hands-on demo stations, a startup and investor networking village with pitching slots, and a hackathon. A "VisionAIres" programme gave director-level attendees a dedicated lounge and content track.

Workshops & Masterclasses

Hands-on sessions run by exhibitors and partners, most fully booked well ahead of the week

The masterclass programme was dominated by big-vendor sessions on applied AI. AWS ran "Build your Agent Factory," a hands-on AgentOps session; AMD covered building an open AI ecosystem; and Barclays paired with NVIDIA for a session on frontier models and agentic decision-making in financial services. Deloitte drew on Formula 1 for a masterclass on enterprise transformation, Lloyds Banking Group ran a Responsible AI masterclass, and DigitalOcean offered a "deploy AI in five minutes" inference challenge. Most of these were fully booked or invite-only well before the event started — a reasonable signal of how much appetite there was for practical, vendor-led AI content over the more general keynote material.

Networking & the LTW Party

The official party, plus a packed independent fringe calendar

The official London Tech Week Party ran Monday 8 June, 7:00–10:30pm, at Between the Bridges on the Southbank (early access from 6:30pm for speakers, VIPs, and sponsors), with a live DJ and a separate VIP area for premium pass holders. Uber sponsored the party for 2026.

Alongside that, London Tech Week's independently run fringe calendar was enormous — dozens of receptions, pitch nights, and community meetups spread across the week, from national-delegation drinks (Hong Kong, Estonia, Indonesia, Taiwan, Ghana) to investor mixers and sector-specific roundtables, most hosted off-site by companies, embassies, and community groups rather than by the festival itself.
