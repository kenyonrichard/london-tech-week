Fringe Events & Networking

The side events, workshops, receptions, and informal gatherings that complemented the main programme.

Workshops & Masterclasses

Hands-on learning across every track

Beyond the main stage, London Tech Week hosted dedicated masterclasses ranging from AI safety best practices to quantum computing. These smaller sessions offered deeper engagement with practitioners and regulators.

The most impactful was a half-day masterclass on deploying agentic AI systems in regulated environments, led by ServiceNow's Chief Learning Officer. Attendees worked through real-world compliance scenarios.

Networking & the LTW Party

Evening events across London's iconic venues

The fringe networking programme was as valuable as the main conference. The London Tech Week Party (19:00–22:30 each evening) brought together speakers, founders, and investors. Additional evening receptions at the Gherkin, the Shard, Somerset House, and a rooftop terrace in Shoreditch provided informal settings for deeper conversations.

These events yielded several actionable connections, including an introduction to a leading climate tech VC and a potential partnership with a European deep-tech incubator.

Key Connections Made

Relationships established during fringe events.

  • Climate Tech VC: Introduced to the lead partner at a £500M climate technology fund. Follow-up meeting scheduled for late June.
  • DeFi Legal Specialists: Connected with a London-based law firm preparing the first sandbox applications. Potential advisory engagement.
  • European Deep-Tech Incubator: Explored co-investment model for quantum and biotech spin-outs from Oxford and Cambridge.
