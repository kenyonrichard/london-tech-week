Conference Programme

Keynotes, breakout sessions, and deep-dive tracks across 7 stages — AI Arena, Core Stage, Transformation, Founders, Deep Tech, Ignition, and EQL:Lounge.

Day 1 - AI Arena Opening

Visionaries Track: AMD, Mayor of London, Perplexity, OpenAI

The conference opened with a keynote speach from the UK Prime Minister Kier Starmer who used the podium to put smart phone developers on notice, he instructed them to work on protecting children online. He also launched new intiatives and commit funding for AI in the UK. This was followed by Dr. Lisa Su (Chair & CEO, AMD) on building the compute foundation for the AI era. Sir Sadiq Khan joined Brent Hoberman CBE and Baroness Martha Lane Fox for a conversation on London's tech leadership.

Darren Hardman (Microsoft UK & Ireland) addressed the future of work, Aravind Srinivas (Perplexity) declared "AI is the Computer," and George Osborne (OpenAI) debated sovereign AI development in Europe alongside Minister Kanishka Narayan MP. The afternoon featured Ron Van Kemenade (Lloyds Banking Group), Stella Li (BYD), and a fireside on autonomous mobility with Uber and Wayve.

Core Stage — Quantum Showcase

Day 1 - Quantum Computing Showcase

The UK's quantum strategy, hardware frontiers, and industry applications

Monday afternoon on Core Stage featured the full Quantum Showcase chaired by Sir Peter Knight. Tom Newby (Head of Office for Quantum, UK Government) outlined the national strategy. Panels covered quantum software (Elham Kashefi, NQCC), hardware breakthroughs (IonQ, NVIDIA, Phasecraft), and sector applications across transport, space, and life sciences.

Notable contributions from Niccolo De Masi (IonQ), Sabrina Maniscalco (Algorithmiq), and Bob Coecke (University of Oxford) on the path from research to commercial quantum advantage.

Day 2 - Applied AI & Enterprise

Wayve, Notion, Liz Kendall MP, Pat Gelsinger, GCHQ

Day 2 opened with Anne Keast-Butler (Director, GCHQ) on AI-driven approaches to national security, followed by Alex Kendall (Wayve) on embodied AI for autonomous vehicles and Ivan Zhao (Notion) on managing knowledge work in the AI age. The Rt Hon Liz Kendall MP, Secretary of State for Science, Innovation & Technology, delivered a keynote address reaffirming the government's commitment to UK AI leadership, outlining new investment in sovereign AI capabilities and AI skills programmes.

Pat Gelsinger (Former CEO, Intel & General Partner, Playground Global) joined a panel on backing the next generation of UK AI, emphasising the need for domestic hardware and compute infrastructure to reduce dependency on foreign supply chains. David Lammy MP, Foreign Secretary, addressed the geopolitical dimensions of AI governance, calling for international collaboration on AI safety standards and ethical frameworks. The afternoon featured parallel tracks across the Founders, Deep Tech, and Ignition stages covering enterprise resilience, marketing & sales, and capital & growth.

Deep Tech Stage — Life Sciences

Day 3 - Life Sciences & Planet

AI in biotech, climate, and space: GSK, Unilever, Sierra, Planet

The final day of the conference focused on deep science and enterprise resilience. Clay Bavor (Co-Founder, Sierra) discussed AI agent design for enterprise, while Sam Kini (Unilever) and Shobie Ramakrishnan (GSK) presented on AI-driven digital transformation in consumer goods and life sciences.

Will Marshall (Co-Founder & CEO, Planet) closed the morning with a keynote on space-based environmental monitoring. The afternoon featured dedicated tracks on SpaceTech & Robotics, Tech for Humanity, and Intelligence 2.0 across the Deep Tech and Ignition stages.
