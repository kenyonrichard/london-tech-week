Conference Programme

The main stage keynotes, breakout sessions, and deep-dive tracks that defined the week.

Opening Keynote

The Mayor of London & Industry Leaders

The conference opened with a rousing keynote from the Mayor of London, who announced a new £250M innovation fund aimed at scaling deep tech startups across the capital. The message was clear: London intends to remain Europe's preeminent tech hub.

Followed by a fireside chat with the CEOs of DeepMind and Darktrace, discussing the UK's unique position at the intersection of world-class research and commercial ambition.

AI & Machine Learning Track

Foundation models, agentic AI, and safety frameworks

A packed two-day track covering the latest in multimodal models, reinforcement learning from human feedback at scale, and the emerging regulatory landscape for frontier AI systems.

Notable sessions included DeepMind's technical deep-dive on their Gemini 2 architecture and a provocative panel on whether open-source AI models pose systemic safety risks.

Conference Schedule

The sessions we prioritised across the week.

Day 1 — Monday, June 8
Opening Keynote & AI Super Session
Main auditorium: Mayor's address, DeepMind keynote, and the flagship AI panel featuring Microsoft Research, Google Brain, and Anthropic.
Day 2 — Tuesday, June 9
Climate Tech Summit & Sustainability Track
Full-day programme on net-zero innovation, carbon markets, and clean energy. Highlights included the Net Zero Innovation Showcase and a workshop on carbon accounting standards.
Day 3 — Wednesday, June 10
Fintech & Open Banking Summit
Regulators, incumbents, and disruptors debated the future of programmable money. The FCA announced a forthcoming DeFi regulatory sandbox.
