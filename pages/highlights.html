Event Highlights

Detailed coverage of the key sessions, demos, and encounters from the week.

AI Super Session

Foundation Models at Scale — DeepMind & Microsoft Research

The opening AI super session packed the main auditorium. DeepMind demonstrated their latest multimodal model capable of reasoning across text, images, code, and scientific data with unprecedented accuracy.

Microsoft Research followed with a deep dive into agentic AI workflows — systems that can plan, execute, and iterate on complex multi-step tasks. The key takeaway: 2026 is the year AI moves from chatbots to autonomous agents in production environments.

Climate Tech & Net Zero

Carbon capture, grid storage, and sustainable finance

The sustainability track was the most energetic of the week. Eight startups pitched novel approaches to direct air capture, with one demonstrating a modular unit that reduces energy consumption by 60% compared to current methods.

Grid-scale storage solutions using iron-air and sodium-ion batteries generated significant investor interest. A dedicated workshop on carbon accounting standards highlighted the growing push for regulatory alignment across the EU and UK.

Fintech & Embedded Finance

Programmable money, DeFi regulation, and real-time payments

Wednesday's fintech summit brought together regulators, incumbents, and disruptors. The headline: the UK's FCA is preparing a regulatory sandbox specifically for DeFi protocols, a first among G7 economies.

Embedded finance continues its march into every vertical — from retail to logistics to healthcare. Revolut's head of B2B banking announced a new API suite for embedded lending, signalling a major play in SME financial infrastructure.

Quantum Computing Lab Tour

Quantum Computing & Biotech

Lab-to-market breakthroughs in deep science

Thursday was devoted to deep science. Oxford Ionics demonstrated a trapped-ion quantum processor with error rates low enough for practical error correction — a milestone many thought was years away.

At the Francis Crick Institute, we toured labs using AI to accelerate drug discovery. One platform reduced candidate screening from 18 months to 6 weeks by combining reinforcement learning with automated wet-lab experimentation.

Startup Demo Day

The next generation of European tech

The competition finals featured 15 startups across AI, climate, health, and fintech. The winner — a London-based startup using AI to optimise urban energy grids — took home £500k in funding and a spot in Google's Startup Accelerator.

We identified six startups as potential strategic partners or investment targets. Detailed profiles and follow-up meeting notes are available in the full report.
