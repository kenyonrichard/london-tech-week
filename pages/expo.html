Startup Village

250+ early-stage companies showcasing cutting-edge solutions

The expo floor was anchored by the Startup Village, a dedicated hall featuring hundreds of early-stage companies from across Europe and beyond. Each startup had a compact booth with live demonstrations, making it easy to cycle through many companies in a single session.

We met a random selection of early growth-stage startups on the floor, spanning AI, quantum, climate tech, fintech, and life sciences — informal conversations rather than a structured meeting schedule.

Corporate Pavilions

AMD, Microsoft, AWS, Dell, and more

Major tech companies mounted impressive pavilions showcasing their latest platforms and partnerships. AMD demonstrated next-generation AI compute infrastructure. Microsoft showcased Azure AI services and Copilot for regulated industries.

AWS highlighted their Amazon Braket quantum computing service, Dell ran a McLaren Racing AI session, and ServiceNow demonstrated agentic AI for enterprise workflows. HubSpot and IFS also had prominent presences on the expo floor.

Exhibits

Big machines and hands-on demos on the show floor

Beyond the booths and pitch decks, several exhibitors used full-scale hardware to draw a crowd. Atlassian's partnership with Williams Racing put an F1 car on the floor a short walk from Barclays' own racing-themed competition stand, while Deloitte ran a racing simulator rig with a live leaderboard that drew a steady queue throughout the week.

AMD showed off its Instinct compute hardware alongside a close-up chip display, and two very different vehicles made the case for AI and electrification in transport: a BYD electric bus in Transport for London livery, and a low-slung hydrogen-powered concept car.
