Startup Village

250+ early-stage companies showcasing cutting-edge solutions

The expo floor was anchored by the Startup Village, a dedicated hall featuring hundreds of early-stage companies from across Europe and beyond. Each startup had a compact booth with live demonstrations, making it easy to cycle through many companies in a single session.

We conducted structured 30-minute meetings with 25 selected startups across AI, quantum, climate tech, fintech, and life sciences. Detailed profiles and evaluation scores are available in the full report.

Corporate Pavilions

AMD, Microsoft, AWS, Dell, and more

Major tech companies mounted impressive pavilions showcasing their latest platforms and partnerships. AMD demonstrated next-generation AI compute infrastructure. Microsoft showcased Azure AI services and Copilot for regulated industries.

AWS highlighted their Amazon Braket quantum computing service, Dell ran a McLaren Racing AI session, and ServiceNow demonstrated agentic AI for enterprise workflows. HubSpot and IFS also had prominent presences on the expo floor.
