VIP Experience

Exclusive access to the VIP programme, premium networking, and private sessions at London Tech Week 2026.

VIP Village

Curated networking for senior leaders and investors

The VIP Village offered an exclusive space for senior executives, investors, and government officials to connect away from the main expo floor. Located in a dedicated area of the Queen Elizabeth II Centre, the village hosted private meeting rooms, a lounge, and curated one-to-one sessions.

The LTW Connect 1:1 meeting service facilitated introductions between attendees based on strategic fit, resulting in a series of high-value conversations across AI, quantum, climate tech, and deep science.

Premium Pass Benefits

What the VIP and Premium passes included

  • VIP Lounge Access — Private workspace and meeting area with catering
  • LTW Connect — Curated 1:1 meetings with strategic matches
  • Exclusive Roundtables — Closed-door sessions with government and industry leaders
  • Premium Seating — Reserved front-row seats at all AI Arena keynotes
  • Evening Receptions — Invitation-only networking at London venues
  • Post-Event Report — Full briefing pack with session summaries and speaker insights

The Event App

A digital badge, agenda and directory in one place

Every attendee got a companion app for the week, built around a personal QR code that doubled as an event badge for check-in and networking. From the home screen it gave one-tap access to a personalised agenda with session-by-session "Add to schedule" buttons, a speaker directory with photos and bios, floor plans, and a live social calendar of fringe events.

The Sponsors & Exhibitors section listed every partner by tier — Headline partners like Microsoft, AWS and Deloitte, Diamond partners like Accenture and AMD — with bookmarking to build a personal shortlist ahead of walking the floor. Smaller touches, including AI-generated session summaries and an accessibility section, rounded out a genuinely useful piece of the VIP experience.
