About the Event

London Tech Week 2026 — Europe's flagship technology festival, held across three days in the heart of the capital.

Event Overview

June 8-10, 2026 | The Olympia, London

London Tech Week returned to The Olympia in Westminster, bringing together over 30,000 attendees, 400+ speakers, and 250+ exhibitors from across the global technology ecosystem. The three-day programme featured 7 concurrent stages spanning the AI Arena, Core Stage, Transformation Stage, Founders Stage, Deep Tech Stage, Ignition Stage, and the EQL: Lounge.

With 21 themed tracks covering visionaries, applied AI, quantum computing, enterprise reinvention, climate tech, space technology, fintech, and founder leadership, the event offered the most comprehensive programme in its history. Keynote speakers included leaders from AMD, Perplexity, OpenAI, Wayve, Notion, Microsoft, AWS, Deliveroo, GSK, Unilever, and senior UK government ministers.

Venue & Stages

Seven stages across The Olympia

  • AI Arena — The main auditorium hosting flagship keynotes and the Visionaries track
  • Core Stage — Quantum Showcase, deep science, and government policy sessions
  • Transformation Stage — Enterprise reinvention, customer operations, and supply chain
  • Founders Stage — Founder leadership, capital & growth, people & culture
  • Deep Tech Stage — Planet & life sciences, data & AI excellence, space tech
  • Ignition Stage — Finance, marketing & sales, tech for humanity
  • EQL:Lounge — Amplifying underrepresented voices, hosted by Tech She Can
