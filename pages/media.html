Media Coverage

How the press covered London Tech Week 2026 — the policy announcements, the royal debut, and the deep tech programme.

Three Storylines

What the coverage actually focused on

Coverage of the week split fairly cleanly into three lanes. UK policy and tech-trade press led on the government's AI investment announcements — Keir Starmer's opening keynote pledges, the Mayor's small-business AI fund, and the corporate money AMD and Nebius put behind UK compute. Prince William's first appearance at the event, chairing a panel on using data to prevent homelessness, was picked up well beyond the tech press — including international broadcasters. And specialist outlets covered the Deep Tech Stage's quantum, space, and robotics programme in more technical depth than the general press bothered with.
