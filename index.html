Trip Timeline

A day-by-day account of the event.

Day 1 — Monday, June 8
Opening Keynotes & AI Arena
Keynote from The Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer
Featuring: Dr. Lisa Su (AMD), Sir Sadiq Khan, Aravind Srinivas (Perplexity), George Osborne (OpenAI)
Day opened with Carolyn Dawson OBE, Dr. Lisa Su on AI compute infrastructure, and the Mayor of London in conversation with Brent Hoberman and Martha Lane Fox. UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer who used the podium to put smartphone developers on notice, he instructed them to work on protecting children online. He also launched new initiatives and committed funding for AI in the UK. Closed with sessions on autonomous mobility with Uber and Wayve. Afternoon Quantum Showcase on Core Stage.
Day 2 — Tuesday, June 9
Applied AI, Enterprise & Founders
Featuring: Deputy Prime Minister David Lammy, Liz Kendall MP, Alex Kendall (Wayve), Ivan Zhao (Notion), Miki Kuusi (Deliveroo)
Secretary of State for Science & Technology addressed the nation's AI ambitions. David Lammy (Deputy Prime Minister and Lord Chancellor) was announcing a major new AI growth project to fast-track legal technology innovation and trial AI assistants in crown courts to reduce case backlogs. Alex Kendall on autonomous vehicle vision, Ivan Zhao on building enduring software companies, and Kipp Bodnar (HubSpot) on AI-driven marketing. Deep Tech and Ignition stages running in parallel.
