Trip Timeline

A day-by-day account of the event.

Day 1 — Monday, June 8
Opening Keynote & AI Track
Sir Keir Starmer announces investment in AI and protecting children online.
Kicked off with a keynote from the Mayor of London on tech investment. Attended the full-day AI track covering foundation models, safety, and regulation.
Day 2 — Tuesday, June 9
Climate Tech & Sustainability
Deep dive into net-zero innovation. Met with 8 early-stage climate startups. Afternoon workshop on carbon accounting standards.
Day 3 — Wednesday, June 10
Fintech & Open Banking Summit
HRH Prince William speaks on businesses responsibilities towards homelessness.
Panel on embedded finance regulation. Networking dinner with fintech founders from Revolut, Monzo, and emerging digital banks.
